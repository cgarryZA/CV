### Head

### Hydrogen Fuel System for Gas Turbines — Year 2 Design Project  
*Team of 5; cryogenic storage, sensing, and safety systems for regional aircraft*

### Body

**Project overview**  
Conceptual design and feasibility study of a **liquid-hydrogen (LH₂) fuel system** for a regional passenger jet (Embraer 190 reference), covering storage, venting, gasification, sensing, and safety. The work focuses on first-pass sizing, operational feasibility, and engineering trade-offs rather than prototype implementation.

**My contributions**  
- Led design of **tank instrumentation and safety subsystems**, including **pressure generation**, **level sensing**, **temperature/pressure monitoring**, and **leak detection**.  
- Contributed to **boil-off and pressure-management logic**, ensuring stable fuel delivery under varying operating conditions.  
- Authored the **Introduction** and **Discussion** sections, framing assumptions, limitations, and next-step validation requirements.  
- Reviewed related sections on **tank insulation**, **structural cut-outs**, and **line sizing** to ensure consistency across subsystems.

**Engineering basis**  
- Applied **Breguet range estimation** and hydrogen-specific energy scaling to size LH₂ mass and storage volume for a ~2000 km mission.  
- Used **NTU-based heat exchanger analysis** to size gasification hardware and identify low-speed operational constraints.  
- Explicitly documented assumptions, sensitivities, and limitations to guide future validation work.

**Outcome**  
Delivered a coherent **end-to-end LH₂ fuel system concept** with checkable sizing calculations, safety logic, and operational considerations. Final project grade: **77%**.
