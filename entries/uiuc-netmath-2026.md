### Mathematics Modules (NetMath) — University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
*Apr 2026 – Dec 2026 | Credit-bearing, exam-assessed | Studied alongside the MSc*

Completing **credit-bearing, exam-assessed** mathematics modules through the UIUC **NetMath** program, focused on **rigorous proof-based analysis and foundational mathematical structures** — deepening the analytical foundations underpinning stochastic modelling, optimisation, and quantitative finance.

### Body

**Modules**
- **MATH 314 — Introduction to Higher Mathematics** *(in progress; current average 100%)* — logic, set theory, ε–δ arguments, and proof techniques (induction, contradiction), with applications to combinatorics and number theory.
- **MATH 447 — Real Analysis (Real Variables)** *(upcoming)* — sequences & convergence (Bolzano–Weierstrass, limsup/liminf), metric spaces, compactness, continuity, differentiation, and Riemann integration.

**Focus**
Emphasis on **rigorous proof construction, abstraction, and the analytical frameworks** that underpin stochastic modelling, optimisation, and quantitative finance — complementing the applied/computational emphasis of the MSc with formal mathematical depth.

**Skills:** Mathematical proofs · real analysis · metric spaces · discrete mathematics.
