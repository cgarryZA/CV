\section*{Research}

\begin{onecolentry}
\textbf{Deep BSDEs for Mean-Field Market Making --- MSc Dissertation, Durham University} \textperiodcentered{} \textit{2026}\\[2pt]
\textit{Quantitative finance $\times$ stochastic control $\times$ machine learning \textperiodcentered{} \href{https://github.com/cgarryZA/MFG-BSDE-Equilibrium}{github.com/cgarryZA/MFG-BSDE-Equilibrium}}
\end{onecolentry}
\vspace{0.06 cm}
\begin{onecolentry}
\begin{highlights}
  \item Built \textbf{models for pricing and quoting} in the Cont--Xiong dealer market-making game --- intensity-controlled fills, inventory risk, mean-field competition --- using deep backward-SDE-with-jumps solvers; \textbf{validated to 0.135\% spread / 0.216\% value error} against an exact benchmark and \textbf{scaled to \textasciitilde{}5,000 agents} (mean-field rate O(1/$\surd$N) confirmed) where grid/PDE methods are infeasible.
  \item \textbf{Studied trading-strategy learning dynamics:} trained \textbf{20 seeded multi-agent RL (decentralised MADDPG)} agents on the same game $\rightarrow$ population mean spread \textbf{1.866} (95\% CI [1.654, 2.072]), above both the competitive Nash anchor (1.515) and the cartel level (1.593); \textbf{t=3.22, p=0.0022}, Cohen's d=0.72, surviving Bonferroni \& Holm correction. Established that the game has a \textit{unique} competitive equilibrium with no supra-cartel fixed point --- so the effect is a property of the \textbf{learning algorithm}, not the market (direct algorithmic-collusion / surveillance relevance: FCA, PRA, SR 11-7).
  \item \textbf{Feature/architecture engineering for financial-dataset models:} identified and fixed three failure modes (generator bypass, BatchNorm erasure, DeepSets collapse) that silently destroy the population/competition signal in mean-field networks; validated across a \textbf{26-experiment} battery (post-fix, learned competition factor varies 4$\times$ and optimal quotes shift 2$\times$).
  \item Developed an \textbf{a-posteriori error-certification theory} (a computable estimator with two-sided guarantees and a certified stopping rule) and machine-checked its core in \textbf{Lean 4 / Mathlib} (\textasciitilde{}36k LOC, axiom-clean) --- rigour that carries straight into trusting a model's numbers.
  \item Built \textbf{reproducible-research infrastructure}: a CI-guarded data$\rightarrow$results pipeline (build fails if any cited number drifts), a GPU job queue, and remote automated job submission.
\end{highlights}
\end{onecolentry}
